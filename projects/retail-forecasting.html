Retail Demand Forecasting under Stockouts

Researching the gap between observed sales and latent demand when products are unavailable.

← Back to portfolio

Problem

Observed sales can be censored by stockouts, which complicates demand estimation, forecasting, and uncertainty calibration.

Dataset and methods

FreshRetailNet-50K dataset; experiments with time-series forecasting and conformal prediction. The dataset covers 898 stores, 18 cities, and 865 SKUs (verify the selected subset and preprocessing).

Current status

Ongoing research / experimental results. A previously explored TimesNet configuration achieved WAPE around 0.264; report the evaluation split and baseline context before public use.
